BEFORE THE FIRST CUP

Good questions.

Can I run it myself, or pay you to set it up?+

The launch has two paths: an open-source edition you run yourself, and a paid managed subscription with setup and supported integrations handled for you. Printer, paper and ink are separate. Managed pricing, supported models and launch availability will be announced.

Will I just connect my tools and go?+

That is the experience we are building: authorize your sources, choose your mix and pair your printer. Instant setup is a product goal, not a currently available service. Some sources may need an import, a local companion or provider approval.

What if I only want things I find interesting?+

Your own life leads, and your interests are welcome too. Follow particular writers, news channels, subreddits, hobbies and topics. Add travel photography, recipes or a daily laugh. Work content is optional.

Can my paper include Getty Images?+

Licensed editorial photography is part of the vision. A Getty connection would require approved API access and image rights covering your use. It is not connected in this preview, and Getty licensing is not promised as part of the subscription. The preview photograph is AI-generated.

How would a daily meme work?+

Choose a humor style—dry, absurd or wholesome—and the planned AI editor would make a daily original. The examples on this page are prewritten concept samples, not live generations.

Are these integrations available now?+

The connection gallery is our roadmap, not a list of live native integrations. Provider capabilities, permissions and availability vary. The product is in development; the editing studio and private accounts are not publicly available.

What am I signing up for?+

The existing MaxShip newsletter, including updates about Folio AM. There is no payment, printer order or automatic subscription purchase. The signup takes place on MaxShip, and you can unsubscribe there at any time.
